Lesson 7, Part 1 – English in Action

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Saying what you like and don't like

Welcome, Junior Zoo Reporter! Visit the animals, collect tickets and become a Zoo Fan Club member. Choose any ticket to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to learn how to say what you like and don't like; to practise stressed syllables in words.
Target language: I'm crazy about…; I find … really interesting; I quite like…; I don't mind…; I'm not keen on…; I don't like … at all; I can't stand…
Vocabulary: dolphin, giraffe, jellyfish, gorilla, kangaroo, alligator, koala, elephant.
Teacher's Book: Lesson 7, parts 1 and 2, pages 115–116 (Class Book: English in action, pages 52–53).
GSE: Reading 34/38 – Listening 38 – Speaking 29.
